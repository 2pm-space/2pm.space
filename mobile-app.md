# Get the mobile app

> Install the 2pm mobile app: iPhone and iPad from the App Store, Android as a direct APK download. Step-by-step, with what to tap when your phone warns you.

Your team's customer conversations on your phone — every channel in one shared inbox, with the full history, photos and files. iPhone and iPad install from the App Store; Android installs directly from us while the Play listing is in review. Pick your phone below; it takes about a minute.

## iPhone & iPad

*App Store · iOS 15.1+*

1. Open the App Store link below **on the iPhone or iPad itself**, or search the App Store for **2pm.space**.
2. Tap **Get**, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your Apple Account password. 2pm lands on your home screen like any other app.
3. Sign in with the email and password you use on 2pm.space.

[Open on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/2pm-space/id6805107582)

Free, no invite needed. Updates arrive through the App Store like any other app. If you installed an earlier build through TestFlight, install this one over it.

## Android

*Direct download · Android 7.0+*

1. Tap **Download the APK** below, on the phone you want the app on. It is about 90 MB, so use Wi-Fi if you can.
2. Your browser will say the file **can harm your device** — every app that does not come from the Play Store gets that warning. Choose **Download anyway**.
3. Open the downloaded file. Android asks to allow installs from this source: tap **Settings** → **Allow from this source**, go back, then **Install**.
4. Sign in with the email and password you use on 2pm.space.

[Download the APK (v1.0.1)](https://2pm.space/mobile-app/android.apk)

Hosted on 2pm.space and signed by us. Nothing else needs to change on the phone — the permission in step 3 applies to this app alone.

## Before you sign in

2pm is a companion to the platform at 2pm.space, not a separate product: your account and your workspaces are the same on both. If you have never signed in, ask whoever runs your workspace to send you an invitation first — the app has no sign-up of its own.

## Why is the Android app not on Google Play yet?

It is on its way through review. Until it is listed, Android installs the APK directly from 2pm.space. iPhone and iPad are already on the App Store.

## Is the Android APK safe to install?

It is built from the same source as the version we submit to Google Play, signed by us, and served from 2pm.space. Android warns about any app that did not arrive from the Play Store; the warning is about where the file came from, not about the file. One thing to know for later: Google re-signs what a developer uploads, so when the Play listing goes live your phone may ask you to remove this build before installing the store one.

## Do I need a separate account for the app?

No. Sign in with the same email and password you use on 2pm.space, and you land in the same workspaces. If you do not have an account yet, ask the person who runs your workspace to invite you.

## Something went wrong

If the App Store link will not open, the download stalls, or the app refuses your sign-in, message us on [Telegram](https://t.me/+SyUCAONs17QxNzU1) and tell us which phone and which step. We usually reply within one business day.
