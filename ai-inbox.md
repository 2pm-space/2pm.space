## From the first message to the thank-you

A customer writes on any channel. The agent consults, takes the order, books the delivery and says thank you — and when the conversation turns, it knows which way to go.

### 2pm AI consults

One agent behind every channel, drawing on everything your workspace knows.

- **Knowledge** — Answers from your documents and catalogue, product photos included
- **Skills** — Your sales playbook — what to ask, what to offer, how to sound
- **Tools** — Looks up products, past orders and shipping fees mid-conversation
- **Memory** — Remembers what the customer told it the last time
- **Customer 360** — Every channel the customer has used, as one history
- **Guardrails** — Keeps every reply inside what it may say and do

1. **Process** — Confirms the items, the phone number and the delivery address back with the customer. `address saved`
2. **Create the order** — A sales order priced from your catalogue — a draft your team confirms, or submitted directly. `SO-2026-48213`
3. **Book the COD shipment** — Booked with Viettel Post with the amount to collect on delivery, or kept on a delivery note for your own carrier. `COD · tracking code`
4. **Say thank you** — When the delivery is reported, an event rule sends a thank-you — written from the thread, or from your template. `delivered → thanked`

**Along the way**

- **If they share a phone number → Friend them on Zalo.** The number is pinned to the contact and your personal Zalo sends a friend request — paced, between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. When they accept, the Zalo chat joins the same customer. `+84 ••• 412 → Zalo`
- **If they go quiet → Care follows up.** A care agent reads the thread and follows up after the silence you set — inside your quiet hours, capped per person, and within the channel’s messaging window. `quiet 2h → follow-up`
- **If they ask for a person → Your team steps in.** The agent sends your team a push alert that opens the thread. Anyone can reply there — or pause AI to keep the thread for themselves. `alert → AI paused`
- **If they spam or abuse → The agent stops replying.** It switches AI and care off for that thread and leaves your team a one-line reason. Anyone can switch it back on from the inbox. `AI off · reason noted`

## From a connected Page to a working agent

Three steps, and the first conversation is answered the same day.

1. **Connect a channel** — Link a Facebook Page, a Zalo account, a Telegram bot or the website widget. Existing history is backfilled, so the inbox opens with the conversations you already have rather than an empty list.
2. **Put an agent behind it** — Choose which agent answers on which channel and give it the documents and tools it needs. It replies on its own once a customer's burst of messages ends — you set how long it waits, which tagged conversations it leaves to your team, and a sandbox mode that keeps its replies in-app while you test.
3. **Watch, step in, improve** — Every reply keeps its run — the tool calls and the cost — behind the execution log. Pause AI on the threads that need a person, and rate or improve a reply: the correction improves the agent now and trains it later.

## Built for teams who actually work the inbox

Not a chatbot bolted to a Page — a shared queue your team and your agents work together.

### An agent on the front line

Point one of your agents at a channel and it answers in your voice, using your product catalogue, your documents and everything it remembers about that customer. It has your tools too, so it can look an order up rather than apologise for not knowing.

### Handover that is not a fight

Any teammate can reply mid-conversation — same thread, same history — and their reply cancels the answer the agent was still writing, without switching it off. Pause AI hands a thread to your team until someone enables it again. Where a person has to decide, the agent leaves it to one: an order it takes waits as a draft for your team to submit, unless you let it confirm orders itself, and it can send your team a push alert that opens the thread.

### One customer, not five threads

Contacts are matched across channels, so the Facebook comment, the Messenger reply and the Zalo message are one person with one history — with their phone numbers, tags and attributes attached.

### Phone numbers, caught and kept

Numbers customers type mid-conversation are detected, deduplicated against the ones you already hold, and pinned to the contact — with the noise (staff numbers, numbers in a forwarded quote) curated out rather than counted.

### Care follow-ups that read the thread

Conversations that go quiet get followed up by an agent that has read the history — inside the quiet hours you set, capped at how often one person may be contacted, and never as an identical blast.

### Numbers you can act on

Volume, response times, and per-channel breakdowns, precomputed by the hour so the dashboard opens instantly instead of scanning a year of messages every time someone changes the date range.

## Questions about the AI inbox

What teams ask before they point their first Page at an agent.

### Which messaging channels can I connect?

Facebook Messenger (including comments on your Page posts), Telegram, personal Zalo logged in by QR code, Pancake, and a chat widget you embed on your own website, with Zalo Official Account coming soon. Anything else connects through the Custom API channel, which is a plain webhook in and a plain webhook out — no new code on our side.

### Does the AI reply on its own, or do my staff still answer?

Both. The agent answers each new message on its own, once the customer's burst of messages ends, and your team can reply in the same thread at any time — a teammate's reply cancels the answer the agent was still writing, and the AI stays on. Pause AI hands a thread to your team (it also pauses care follow-ups there) until someone enables it again, and conversations carrying a tag you pick can be left to your team entirely. There is no approval step mid-conversation; for orders, the checkpoint is the order itself — the agent creates a draft your team submits, unless you let it confirm orders on its own.

### Do conversations from the same customer stay together?

Yes. Conversations are linked on a shared phone number or email, so someone who comments on a Facebook post and later messages you on Zalo is shown as one customer with one history — including phone numbers detected in the conversation, custom attributes your team fills in, and any profile data pulled from your own systems.

### What happens to images, voice notes and files?

They are stored with the conversation and shown inline — photos, albums, stickers, files, locations, polls and product cards all render as themselves rather than as a broken link. Voice messages are transcribed automatically, so a voice note is searchable text your agent can actually read and answer.

### Can I control which staff see which channels?

Yes. Channel access is assigned per member, per role, or per team, so an agency can give one client team access to one Page without exposing the rest of the workspace. Everything else in the workspace uses the same permission system.

### Can the inbox follow up with customers who go quiet?

Yes — that is what Care does. You set the trigger (a conversation idle for N hours, a tag, a filter), the quiet hours it must never send inside, and a cap on how many times one person can be contacted. The follow-up itself is written by an agent using the conversation history, not a fixed template blast.
