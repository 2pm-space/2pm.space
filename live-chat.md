## Questions about live chat

What teams ask before they put an agent on their website.

### How do I put it on my website?

Create a Website Widget channel, style it, and paste the one script tag it gives you into your site. The tag carries the channel's embed token, and you can restrict it to the domains you list, so a copied snippet does not start answering on someone else's site.

### Does the AI answer everything, or can my team reply?

Both. The channel's agent answers as soon as a visitor writes, with a typing indicator while it works. Every website conversation also lands in the same inbox as Messenger, Telegram and Zalo, where any teammate can reply — and Pause AI stops the agent answering that thread until someone turns it back on.

### Can I collect contact details before the chat starts?

Yes. The pre-chat form takes up to 20 fields — name, email, phone, short or long text, a select or a checkbox — and can be made skippable. What the visitor fills in is attached to the contact in the inbox, alongside the page they arrived on, the page they are on now, their browser and their language.

### Will it match my brand?

The title, greeting, welcome messages, colours, theme, launcher icon, agent name and avatar are all yours to set, and the launcher can be your own image. Placement is set per device: any corner, with its own offsets and sizes on desktop, tablet and mobile, and a full-screen sheet on phones.

### Which languages does it come in?

The widget's own text is available in English, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, French and Lao, or it can follow the visitor's browser. What the agent replies in is up to its instructions — most teams tell it to answer in the language the customer writes in.

### What does it cost?

The widget is included, like every other feature, with no per-seat or per-widget fee. What you pay for is the AI the agent uses to answer, billed at the model provider's own rate.
