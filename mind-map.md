## How to make a mind map online

Three steps, no setup, and nothing to install.

1. **Start from a blank canvas** — Draw straight on the live demo above, or create a mindmap in your workspace. No download, no template to hunt through — you get an empty canvas and a root idea.
2. **Branch out your thinking** — Add child ideas, drag branches into a better order, and reach for drawings, shapes or connectors when a branch needs more than a line of text.
3. **Share it and edit together** — Send a link with view, comment or edit rights. Collaborators see each other on the canvas and every change lands instantly — no refresh, no merge conflicts, no exported copies to reconcile.

## What teams use mind maps for

A mind map is the fastest way to get the shape of an idea out of one head and in front of everyone else. These are the maps people keep coming back to on 2pm.space.

### Brainstorming and workshops

Open a blank canvas at the start of a call and let everyone add branches at the same time instead of taking turns dictating to whoever is sharing their screen. Because the mindmap is live, the session ends with the thinking already organised — no one has to transcribe a whiteboard photo the next morning, and the people who joined late can read back how the map grew.

### Project and product planning

Break a goal into workstreams, hang the tasks and open questions off each branch, and keep the whole shape visible instead of buried in a list that only shows ten rows at a time. Tabs let a roadmap, a risks map and a research map live inside one mindmap, so the context behind a decision stays next to the decision rather than in a different file.

### Study notes and revision maps

Turning a chapter into a mind map forces its structure into the open: the main idea in the middle, arguments as branches, evidence as leaves. Nothing is capped and nothing is charged, so a whole semester of notes can live in one workspace, and a study group can work through the same map by link without each person paying for a seat.

### Research and knowledge maps

Map a subject while you read it — sources, claims, the gaps between them — and the map becomes a shared reference instead of a personal scratchpad. It also sits in the same Drive that AI agents on 2pm.space read for knowledge, so a map built to think with can go on to answer questions on its own.

## Mind mapping questions, answered

What people ask before they move their first map over.

### Is the mindmap maker really free?

Yes. Mind mapping on 2pm.space is free with no cap on nodes, no cap on how many mindmaps you keep, and no paywall in front of sharing or collaboration. The live demo on this page needs no account and no credit card — you can start drawing on it right now.

### Can several people edit the same mind map at once?

Yes. Every mindmap is a shared document, so edits from different people merge on their own and there is no save button to forget. You see collaborators move around the canvas as they work, and a browser that drops its connection replays your changes when it comes back instead of losing them.

### Do I need an account to try it?

No. The editor above is a real, live mindmap — whatever you draw is shared with other visitors on this page in real time. An account only matters once you want a mindmap of your own, kept privately, with control over who may open it.

### How do I share a mind map with someone outside my team?

Share it by link with view, comment, or edit access. Someone who only needs to read the map does not have to create an account, so a mindmap can go straight into a client email or a public page without a sign-up wall in front of it.

### How big can one mind map get?

There is no node limit, and panning and zooming stay smooth at thousands of nodes. When one canvas stops being the right container, split the topic across tabs inside the same mindmap rather than scattering it over separate files — related maps then stay in one document.

### Can I draw on the canvas, not just add branches?

Yes. Freehand drawing, shapes, connectors, images and free-floating text live on the same canvas as the mind map branches. A diagram that outgrows the strict parent-and-child shape does not have to move to a second tool.

### Can AI agents use my mindmaps?

Mindmaps sit in the same collaborative Drive as your documents and tables — the knowledge base agents built on 2pm.space read from. A planning or research map can therefore become context an agent answers with, with no export step in between.

### Does it work on any device?

It runs in any modern browser with nothing to install and no plugin to approve. The canvas takes mouse, trackpad and touch input, so a map opened on a tablet stays readable and navigable.
