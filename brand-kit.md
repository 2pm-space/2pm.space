## Set up a brand in three steps

Name it, brief it, put it to work.

1. **Name it** — Create the brand and link the fanpages it posts and replies from.
2. **Brief it** — Fill in the voice and the design, attach product shots and logos, link the documents it may quote, and pick the products it is about.
3. **Put it to work** — Pick the brand on the Content Calendar, in Magic Studio or on an agent, and everything they write or draw starts from the brief.

## Everything a brand is, in one place

So the tenth piece sounds and looks like the first.

### A voice that holds

Tone, language, audience, keywords and guidelines — "friendly, expert", "avoid medical claims" — read by every writer before it drafts.

### Design that image models follow

Name the colours in your palette, describe the visual style, the photography and the composition, and list what always and never appears. The Avoid list is appended to every image prompt made under the brand.

### Pictures it's allowed to use

Product shots, logos and style references, each labelled. Attach any of them as a reference in Magic Studio without uploading it again.

### Documents it can quote

Link the shipping policy, the FAQ, the manuals and the brand book from your Drive. When a piece is written, the passages that match are handed to the writer.

### The products it sells

Choose from the workspace catalogue, or leave it empty for the whole catalogue. The planner proposes topics for them, the writer quotes their specs, the illustrator reproduces their pictures.

### Ready to publish

Fixed footers — address, hotline, hashtags — appended when a post goes out, content statuses in the brand's own words, and sign-off on the statuses that need it.

## Brand Kit questions

What people ask before they set up their first brand.

### What uses the brand?

The Content Calendar plans, writes and illustrates from it, Magic Studio draws with its design and images, and agents can be linked to it. One brand serves all of them.

### Can I run several brands in one workspace?

Yes. Each brand has its own voice, design, images, documents, products, footers and fanpages, and you can share each one only with the members who work on it.

### How does the design reach image generation?

The palette, visual style, photography, composition and Always / Never rules brief every image made under the brand, and the Avoid field is appended to each image prompt.

### Where do the products come from?

From the workspace's product catalogue. A brand chooses among them; if it chooses none, it works from the whole catalogue.

### What is a post footer?

A fixed block — address, hotline, slogan, hashtags — appended under a post when it is published, never written into the post itself. A brand can keep several and mark one as the default.
